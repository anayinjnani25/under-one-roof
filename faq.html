Care

Dust gently and spot clean only. Keep pieces out of strong direct sun so the colours last, and store garlands flat between seasons. Each piece's care notes are on its page, under "The piece".

Put it on after perfume and lotion, keep it dry, wipe it with a soft dry cloth, and store each piece separately in its pouch so fringes don't tangle. Avoid polishing oxidised pieces; it removes the dark finish.

A toran has a loop at each end: two small hooks or nails at the top corners of the door frame hold it level. For a garland, fix a hook at each end and let it dip; the closer the hooks, the deeper the swag.
