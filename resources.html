Caring for your pieces

Hanging & sizing

01

Measure the doorway

Measure across the top of the door frame, inside edge to inside edge. A toran usually spans the full width or sits just inside it, so its flaps frame the opening without catching on the door.

Not sure a piece will fit? Ask us for its exact measurements before you order.

02

Hang a toran

Every toran has a loop at each end. Two small hooks or nails at the top corners of the frame hold it level. Removable adhesive hooks work well on painted frames and rented homes.

Hang it with the band level first, then let the flaps settle for a day.

03

Drape a garland

Fix a hook at each end and let the garland dip between them. The closer the hooks, the deeper the swag. Pom-pom and leaf garlands can also be wound loosely around a banister or mirror.

For a three-swag garland, add hooks where the tassels meet so each swag hangs evenly.

04

Place a wall or door hanging

One nail or hook is enough. On a wall, hanging the centre of the piece at roughly eye level (about 145–150 cm from the floor) feels natural. On a door, keep the strands clear of the handle.

Bead and bell strands can twist in transit. Hang the piece and run your fingers down each strand to straighten it.

Wearing jewelry

  • Put it on last. Perfume, lotion and hairspray dull both oxidised and plated finishes. Let them dry before you put your jewelry on.
  • Keep it dry. Take pieces off before bathing, swimming or exercising.
  • Wipe, don't polish. A soft, dry cloth is all you need. Polishing compounds remove the darkened finish on oxidised pieces.
  • Store separately. Keep each piece in its pouch so fringes don't tangle and surfaces don't scratch.
